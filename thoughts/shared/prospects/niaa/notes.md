# NIAA — prospect notes

**Why NIAA first (decided 2026-06-08):**
- They ADMINISTER the IPP — a Commonwealth-wide evidence layer for Indigenous procurement is their remit, not just their procurement need. If they adopt or endorse, every other agency is downstream (multiplier, vs Defence = one hard door).
- 108/132 suppliers certified — the cleanest evidence story in the top 5.
- Smaller, mission-aligned agency — plausibly reachable without a procurement panel position.
- Integrity angle: per-supplier delivery evidence is exactly the data shape IPP integrity work ("black cladding" reviews) needs. Use carefully — frame as capability, never as accusation.

**Risks / open questions:**
- NIAA may view a private evidence layer over THEIR policy as turf. Counter: open registry, attribution-first, we issue no marks.
- Procurement intelligence may need to enter via a panel (e.g. a digital marketplace listing) — investigate before/after first meeting.
- Recent contract titles in our data are PO numbers (untitled) — tender-pack demo category should be picked from their published forward procurement plan instead. TODO before any meeting.
- Find the named IPP/procurement contact — NOT a generic inbox. (NIAA org chart / LinkedIn / annual report.)

**Runner-up packs (build only if NIAA stalls):**
- Defence: 255 suppliers / 216 certified / $2.3B — biggest number in the Commonwealth, dedicated First Nations procurement function, but long sales cycle.
- Services Australia: 174 suppliers / 1,502 contracts — most active, weaker certified ratio (82/174).
