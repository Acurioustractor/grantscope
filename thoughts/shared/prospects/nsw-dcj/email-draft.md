**Subject:** Your 61 Indigenous suppliers, as one evidenced list

---

Hello [name],

I run CivicGraph, which builds public-record evidence on social and Indigenous enterprise supply.

From NSW contract disclosures, DCJ has 91 social and Indigenous enterprises holding 383 disclosed
contracts. 84 of those are still running. 61 of the 91 are Indigenous-registered, 45 through ORIC and
16 through Supply Nation.

I am not telling you anything about your own department you could not find. The point is that finding it
takes a data project, and holding it current takes another one. Nobody publishes it as a single
per-supplier list, so nobody can cite it in a tender evaluation or an Aboriginal Procurement Policy
report without rebuilding it by hand each time.

One thing in it surprised me and might be useful to you. The smallest disclosed contract in the set is
exactly $150,000, which is the disclosure threshold. Everything below it is invisible. That is the same
line the NSW Social Enterprise Policy uses for direct engagement, so the easiest lane in the policy is
the one with no public evidence trail at all. That is a reporting gap rather than a performance one, and
it is the kind of thing we can close.

Would you have 30 minutes? I would show you the list against a procurement you have coming up, and you
can tell me whether it holds up against what you already know.

Ben
