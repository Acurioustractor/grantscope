# Inventory — Shard A (names starting `_`, digit, or `a`–`f`)

Scope: **281 objects** (276 tables + 5 materialized views) out of the 812 public-schema objects.
No object in this shard starts with a digit. One starts with `_`.

Sources used:
- Row counts, kind, bytes: `census.csv` (exact `count(*)`, verified 2026-08-14)
- Columns + types: `columns.csv` (tables) and a direct `pg_attribute` query (the 5 matviews — `information_schema.columns` does not cover matviews)
- Declared FKs: `foreign_keys.csv`
- 5 batched psql spot-check calls covering ~30 of the largest/least-obvious objects (samples + distributions). Everything marked **[spot-checked]** below was read from real rows; everything else is inferred from column names, types and FK edges.

Legend for `join_keys`: type in brackets. `→` means a **declared** FK; `~` means a name/shape match with no declared FK (integration hazard).

---

## The two facts that matter most in this shard

1. **There are two disjoint "entity" universes and they are wired to different crosswalks.**
   - `entity_xref` (1,211,744) is the crosswalk for the **`gs_entities` graph** — `identifier_type` is `GS_ID` / `ABN` / `ACN` / `TRADING_NAME` / `ACNC_ABN`, sourced from `gs_entities`, `abr_registry`, `foundations`. **[spot-checked]**
   - `entity_identifiers` (31,451) is the crosswalk for the **CRM `canonical_entities` universe** — `identifier_type` is `linkedin_id` / `linkedin_url` / `ghl_id` / `email` / `xero_id`. It has a declared FK to `canonical_entities.id`, **not** to `gs_entities`. **[spot-checked]**
   CLAUDE.md's key-table list describes `entity_identifiers` as if it were the graph identifier table. It is not. Anyone joining `entity_identifiers.entity_id` to `gs_entities.id` will silently get zero rows.

2. **`abn` is `text` everywhere in this shard** (`abr_registry`, `acnc_charities`, `asic_companies`, `austender_contracts.supplier_abn`, `foundations.acnc_abn`, `asic_name_lookup.abn`, `ato_tax_transparency.abn`, `community_directory_orgs.abn`, …) — with one loud exception: **`donor_entity_matches.matched_abn` stores `'0'` as a sentinel for "no ABN"** **[spot-checked]**, which will silently match nothing and can be mistaken for a real value.

## Notable clusters

**1. The ABR / ASIC / ASX / ATO corporate-registry spine (24.3 M rows).**
`abr_registry` (20.0 M) · `asic_companies` (2.17 M) · `asic_name_lookup` (2.15 M) · `ato_tax_transparency` (26 K) · `asx_companies` (2 K) · `entity_xref` (1.21 M) · `donor_entity_matches` (10 K).
This is the identity substrate for everything else. All keys are `text`. `asic_name_lookup` is a materialised name index over `asic_companies`, not independent data. `entity_xref` is the only thing that connects any of it to `gs_entities`, and it does so for only 48% of graph entities by ABN.

**2. The ACNC charity stack (525 K rows).**
`acnc_charities` (66 K registry) → `acnc_ais` (360 K annual financials, 2017–2023) → `acnc_programs` (98 K named programs, **carries `gs_entity_id`**) → `charity_impact_reports` (62 LLM-extracted outcome reports) → `charity_claims` (2). Joined by `abn:text` throughout. `acnc_ais_line_items` is an empty alternate shape. Missing FY2024 entirely.

**3. The foundations / philanthropy family (~110 K rows across 12 tables).**
`foundations` (11,159) is the hub, with nine children: `foundation_grantees` (6,001 — actual giving), `foundation_programs` (4,218) → `foundation_program_years` (431), `foundation_category_assignments` (42,599), `foundation_geo_focus` (16,942), `foundation_power_profiles` (10,114), `foundation_people` (33), `foundation_relationship_signals` (55), `foundation_categories` (16), `foundation_notes` (0). Plus two derived scorecards: `funder_intelligence` (1:1 with foundations) and `funder_context_snapshot` (1,038, contaminated with private Xero figures). Plus governance lists `funder_allowlist`/`funder_blocklist` and the warm-path derivation `funder_board_paths` (2,651).
This is the most complete family in the shard and 97% of it is linked to `gs_entities`. It is also where the classifier confidence values look constant rather than computed.

**4. The ALMA evidence family (~24 K rows across 27 `alma_*` tables + 5 matviews).**
Core: `alma_interventions` (2,136) ↔ `alma_evidence` (631) ↔ `alma_outcomes` (2,869) via two junction tables. Supporting: `alma_government_programs` (207), `alma_source_documents` (117), `alma_media_articles` (872), `alma_raw_content`/`alma_discovered_links`/`alma_ingestion_jobs` (ingest), `alma_consent_ledger`/`alma_stories` (consent), five dashboard matviews. `alma_interventions` is the best-connected table in the whole shard (four org links + `gs_entity_id` + inbound FKs from `justice_funding`, `services`, `registered_services`).
Weak point: provenance. 117 documents, 9 citation links.

**5. The civic-accountability family (~13 K rows across 13 `civic_*` tables).**
Inputs: `civic_hansard` (647), `civic_ministerial_statements` (649), `civic_ministerial_diaries` (1,728), `civic_rti_disclosures` (17), `civic_consultancy_spending` (18), `civic_charter_commitments` (75). Classification layer: `civic_org_classifications` (626), `civic_meeting_tags` (160), `civic_funding_yj_classifications` (308). Publication layer: `civic_intelligence_claims` (88) + `civic_claim_evidence` (250) + `civic_alerts` (293) + `civic_metric_snapshots` (16) + `civic_digests` (1). RAG layer: `civic_intelligence_chunks` (7,022 embeddings).
This family has the most mature *governance* design (LLM proposes → human confirms → override reason recorded → claim published with methodology URL) and the least *data*.

**6. The justice / child-protection statistics family — and its holes.**
`crime_stats_lga` (58,125, likely NSW-only) · `dss_payment_demographics` (105,529) · `aihw_child_protection` (2,981) · `children_commissioner_reports` (11) · `cross_system_stats` (148) · `coroners_findings` (39) · `facility_partnerships` (27) · `australian_frameworks` (4) · **`aihw_youth_justice_stats` (13)** · **`alma_funding_data` (2)** · **`bocsar_youth_offending` (0)** · **`abs_indigenous_population_by_lga` (0)**.
The vision names "youth detention numbers" as a pillar. The four tables designed to hold them contain 15 rows between them.

**7. Internal ACT finance and ops (~16 K rows across ~25 tables).**
`bank_statement_lines`, `finance_receipt_documents` (7,172), `finance_receipt_bank_line_links` (4,292), `finance_ai_routing_suggestions`, `dext_*`, `email_financial_documents`, `discovered_subscriptions`, `bookkeeping_*`, `collections_actions`, `cashflow_scenarios`, `financial_*`, `act_payable_decisions`, `act_entities`, `compliance_tracking`, `bgfit_*` (6 tables for one client).
None of this is public data. It shares a database with public-facing tables and several of its rows (`funder_context_snapshot`) are already denormalised into philanthropy tables.

**8. The CRM / contact intelligence family (~55 K rows across ~20 tables).**
`canonical_entities` (15,324) ← `entity_identifiers` (31,451) · `communications_history` (31,961) · `contact_*` (7 tables) · `campaign_*` (7 tables) · `calendar_events` (3,585) · `crm_contact_organization_affiliations` (75) · `contact_entity_links` (643) · `civicscope_act_entity_bridge` (3,074).
Heaviest PII concentration in the shard. Three different contact-key types coexist (`uuid` → `canonical_entities`, `text` → GHL id, `uuid` → `person_identity_map`).

**9. Agent / pipeline operations (~11 K rows across ~15 tables).**
`agent_runs` (6,111) is the real one. Around it: four agent registries, three task queues, three enrichment-review queues, `discoveries` (2,467), `agent_audit_log` (99).

**10. Content / media (~250 rows across ~15 tables).**
`articles` (49) ≈ `blog_posts` (37) · `art_innovation` (7, load-bearing) · `authors` (1) · `events` (13) · `cms_pages` (7) · `editable_content` (19) · `content_*`, `article_*`, `campaign_content`.
The entire "media" pillar of the vision, apart from `alma_media_articles` (872) and `exa_media_mentions` (162), is under 300 rows.

**11. A foreign, integer-keyed app squatting in the DB.**
`app_users` (1), `editable_content` (19), `event_feedback` (2), `fellows` (22), `ce_users` (2), `ce_metrics` (15). camelCase columns, `integer` primary keys, `timestamp without time zone`, and a `password_hash` outside Supabase Auth. These do not belong to either CivicGraph or JusticeHub as documented.
