# Database inventory — shard n–z

**Scope:** every public-schema object in `tednluwflfhxyucgwigh` whose name starts with a letter n–z.
**Count:** 306 objects (296 tables, 10 materialized views). 272 populated, 34 empty — verified against `census.csv`; every row count and kind in the table below was diffed against it programmatically, zero mismatches.
**Row counts:** verbatim from `census.csv` (exact `count(*)`, verified 2026-08-14).
**Columns:** from `columns.csv` / `pg_attribute` (matviews).
**Spot-checks:** ~45 `LIMIT 2-3` reads across 7 batched psql calls, plus 4 redundancy-verification aggregates.
Every claim marked *verified* below was read from live rows; everything else is inferred from column names + FK graph and is labelled as such.

One deliberate deviation from the lifecycle enum: `public_spending_transactions` is marked `scaffold_empty` despite holding 1 row — the single row is a stray, the table has never carried real data.

Lifecycle tally: app_operational 155 · core_source 61 · derived 42 · scaffold_empty 32 · staging 6 · backup 6 · crosswalk 3 · superseded 1.
Domain tally: platform_ops_auth 108 · storytelling_consent 33 · grants_funding 24 · social_services 22 · government_spend_procurement 19 · ai_agents_pipeline 19 · media_narrative 18 · geography_place 17 · people_directors_governance 9 · justice_youth_detention 9 · philanthropy_giving 7 · charities_ngo 7 · corporate_registry 6 · political_influence 4 · evidence_outcomes_alma 4.

**That tally is itself the headline finding: 108 of 306 objects in this shard — a third — are platform/business plumbing, not civic data.**

Shard totals by row count: `political_donations` 2.55M and `privacy_audit_log` 1.28M alone are 75% of the shard's rows.

## Notable clusters

### 1. NDIS quarterly reporting (7 populated + 3 empty, ~419K rows)
`ndis_utilisation` (143,987) · `ndis_active_providers` (134,572) · `ndis_participants` (67,353) · `ndis_registered_providers` (48,510) · `ndis_market_concentration` (14,915) · `ndis_participants_lga` (8,329) · `ndis_compliance_actions` (2,322) · `ndis_first_nations` (1,486) — plus empty `ndis_plan_budgets`, `ndis_providers`, `ndis_sda`.

All ingested from NDIA quarterly public data. Two shapes: **market-slice aggregates** (state × district × disability × age × support class × period) and **provider-level records** (`ndis_registered_providers`, `ndis_compliance_actions`, the only two with `gs_entity_id`). This is a fully-formed disability-services dataset that the CivicGraph documentation does not mention at all. Three traps: `"ALL"` rollup rows mixed with detail; `ndis_registered_providers` is a snapshot-per-day so provider counts must dedupe; `ndis_plan_budgets`/`ndis_providers`/`ndis_sda` duplicate columns that already exist in populated siblings.

### 2. Youth justice / detention evidence (6 objects, ~40K rows)
`rogs_justice_spending` (22,364, the Productivity Commission spend tables, wide format) · `outcomes_metrics` (9,193, published outcome statistics with source URLs) · `qld_watchhouse_snapshot_rows` (8,488) + `qld_watchhouse_snapshots` (201) · `tracker_site_snapshots` (342) + `tracker_evidence_events` (39) · `oversight_recommendations` (139) · `youth_detention_facilities` (21) · empty `youth_survey_results`.

The watchhouse pair is the standout: daily custody counts by facility, age group, Indigenous status and length of stay, with SHA-256 of the source PDF. `rogs_justice_spending` is the money side but needs unpivoting (states are columns). `outcomes_metrics` is the cleanest cross-jurisdiction numbers table in the shard.

### 3. People, directors and identity (6 objects, ~588K rows)
`person_roles` (339,698, registry appointments) → `person_identities` (230,434, disambiguation verdicts, 1:1 on `role_id`) · `person_identity_map` (14,919, CRM people from Gmail/LinkedIn/Notion) → `person_entity_links` (2,571, the only CRM↔graph bridge) · `people` (84, parliamentarians) · `person_role_holdings` (126, curated public roles) · `role_taxonomy` (33).

**These are three unconnected person universes**: registry officers (`person_roles`), CRM contacts (`person_identity_map`), and public figures (`people`/`person_role_holdings`). Only 2,571 links exist between the CRM and the graph, and nothing links `people` to either. 109,264 of 339,698 registry roles have no identity verdict.

### 4. Place and geography (12 objects)
`postcode_geo` (12,299) + **six dated backups** · `seifa_2021` (10,572) · `postcode_sa2_concordance` (7,261) · `sa2_reference` (2,473) · `sa3_regions` (14, badly incomplete) · `nt_communities` (75) · `place_funding_snapshot` (5) · `place_corrections` (1) · three drained `stg_*` staging tables.

Live `postcode_geo` has **fewer** filled LGA codes (11,638) than four of its own backups (11,885–11,959) — deliberate, per the "unplaced rather than confidently wrong" rule, but a trap for anyone reading null as missing.

### 5. Buyer / procurement workflow (14 objects)
`procurement_alerts` (53,223) · `procurement_shortlist_events` (37) · `procurement_workflow_runs` (11) · `procurement_pack_exports` (5) · `procurement_shortlist_items` (5) · `procurement_shortlist_comments` (3) · `procurement_shortlists` (2) · `procurement_tasks` (2) · `procurement_shortlist_watches` (1) · plus 5 entirely empty (`_notification_channels`, `_notification_outbox`, `_pending_team_invites`, `_team_settings`, `_webhook_receipts`).

The whole schema — approval state machine, notification outbox, webhooks, team roles — supports **2 shortlists and 5 shortlist items**. Meanwhile `procurement_alerts` holds 53,223 machine-generated donor↔contractor crossover findings that have nothing to do with any shortlist and have never been delivered anywhere.

### 6. Social enterprise registry + buyer wedge (3 objects)
`social_enterprises` (12,180) → `se_search_index` (12,180, verified strict 1:1 join) → `se_buyer_prospects` (438). The registry has no `gs_entity_id`; it links to the graph only through `abn` text. `se_buyer_prospects` is keyed on buyer **name** with no id.

### 7. Storytelling and consent (~25 objects)
`storytellers` (227) · `transcripts` (208) · `synced_stories` (190) · `public_profiles` (218) · `transcript_analysis` (271) · `quotes` (37) · plus photo/album/tag/media/partner-microsite tables and 5 empty scaffolds.

Every one of these carries consent semantics — `consent_level`, `cultural_protocol`, `story_use_permissions`, `narrative_ownership_level`, `requires_cultural_review`. `transcripts` alone has five independent consent booleans. Several reference the *other* Supabase project (`empathy_ledger_*_id` columns) so no FK can protect them. `photo_storyteller_tags` stores facial-recognition coordinates against identified people.

### 8. ACT internal business operations (~70 objects)
Xero (11 tables incl. `xero_tokens` holding live OAuth secrets) · receipts (4 overlapping tables) · subscriptions (5) · projects/health/financials (~20) · sprints (3) · Notion mirrors (10) · CRM (`relationship_health`, `relationship_pipeline`, `touchpoints`, `supporters_intelligence`, `supporter_comms_summary`) · revenue planning (3).

This is roughly a quarter of the shard by object count and **none of it is civic data**. It is A Curious Tractor's own books, pipeline and staff costs sharing a database with a public-facing civic dataset. Salary figures, bank account numbers, invoice values and OAuth tokens all live here.

### 9. Agent / pipeline infrastructure
`source_frontier` (56,081 — the crawl frontier, the best available map of data provenance) · `site_health_checks` (9,608) · `webhook_delivery_log` (25,792) · `pm2_cron_status` (159) · `sprint_suggestions` (573) · `signal_events`/`signal_content` · `tag_inference_rules`/`tagging_sweep_runs` · `ralph_prds`/`ralph_tasks` · `pipeline_runs`.

`source_frontier` is the single most under-exploited object in the shard: 56K URLs with owning agent, cadence, last HTTP status and failure count — a data-source health dashboard already sitting in a table.

### 10. Operational logs that dominate the row count
`privacy_audit_log` (1,278,440) · `page_views` (38,115) · `webhook_delivery_log` (25,792) · `site_health_checks` (9,608). Together 1.35M rows — **more than half the shard's non-donation rows are logs**, and the largest one retains full request bodies.

## Suspected redundancy

**Confirmed by query**

1. **`se_search_index` is a strict 1:1 derivation of `social_enterprises`** — verified 12,180 of 12,180 rows join. It is a plain table, not a matview, so nothing guarantees it stays in sync.
2. **Six `postcode_geo` backups** (`_lga_backup_20260808`, `_20260809b`, `_20260809c`, `_20260809d`, `_20260809e`, `_state_backup_20260809e`) — ~62K rows of pure cruft. Verified LGA-filled counts: 11,885 / 11,957 / 11,957 / 11,959 / 0 / 11,635. **`_20260809b` and `_20260809c` have identical coverage** — one is provably redundant. Keep at most the earliest (`_20260808`) and delete the rest.
3. **`person_identities` is 1:1 with `person_roles.id`** (230,434 distinct `role_id`) — it could be columns on `person_roles` rather than a second 230K-row table, and 109,264 roles have no row here at all.
4. **`opportunities_unified` is 90% a re-projection of `grant_opportunities`** (16,106 of 17,790 rows).

## Cross-cutting hazards for anyone building on this shard

**Type mismatches that will silently fail to join**

| Column | Type here | Type elsewhere |
|---|---|---|
| `outcome_submissions.gs_entity_id` | **text** | uuid in ndis_*, nz_charities, research_grants, state_tenders, vic_grants_awarded, source_frontier |
| `stories.transcript_id` | **text** | `transcripts.id` uuid |
| `portraits.storyteller_id` | **text** | `storytellers.id` uuid |
| `story_comments.user_id`, `story_reactions.user_id`, `user_gamification_stats.user_id` | **text** | `users.id` uuid |
| `touchpoints.contact_id` | **bigint** | uuid / text everywhere else |
| `notion_projects_cache.alma_intervention_id` | **text** | uuid in `services`, `registered_services` |
| `witta_contributions.approved_by` | **integer** | uuid |
| `procurement_shortlist_items.gs_id` | **text** (`gs_entities.gs_id`) | uuid `gs_entities.id` used by every declared FK |
| `sprint_snapshots.project_id`, `project_media_links.link_id`, `review_media_links.link_id`, `review_videos.link_id`, `pulse_report_links.entity_id`, `relationship_pipeline.entity_id`, `recommendation_outcomes.entity_id` | **text** (polymorphic) | — no FK possible |

Every `abn` in this shard is **text**, which is consistent — but note `person_roles` stores `company_acn` and `company_abn` separately, and `political_donations.donor_abn` was empty on every sampled row.

**Money stored as float:** `v_grant_focus_areas.total_funding` and `v_grant_provider_summary.total_funding` are `double precision`. Everything else uses `numeric`.

**Secrets and sensitive data in tables**
- `xero_tokens` — plaintext OAuth `refresh_token` + `access_token` (2 rows).
- `users.password_hash` — app-level password hashes alongside Supabase Auth.
- `procurement_notification_channels.signing_secret` (empty, but the column exists).
- `subscriptions.current_login_email` / `login_url` — credential-adjacent.
- `xero_bank_accounts.bank_account_number`.
- `team_members.annual_salary` / `hourly_rate`, `project_salary_allocations.monthly_cost` — named-individual pay.
- `privacy_audit_log.query` / `.body` — full request payloads retained indefinitely (1.28M rows).
- `person_identity_map` — 14,919 Gmail-derived contacts with `indigenous_affiliation` and `government_influence` scoring.
- `storytellers`, `transcripts`, `quotes`, `photo_storyteller_tags`, `org_participants`, `org_milestones` — consent-gated personal and cultural content.

**Naming inconsistency:** the ten `v_*` objects are **materialized views**, not regular views, while the other 88 matviews use `mv_`. Anyone scripting refreshes by prefix will miss them.
