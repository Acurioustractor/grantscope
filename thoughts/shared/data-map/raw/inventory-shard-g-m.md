# Inventory — Shard G–M (Supabase `tednluwflfhxyucgwigh`, public schema)

**Scope:** every table/matview whose name starts with a letter g–m (case-insensitive).
**Count:** 225 objects — 142 plain tables, 83 materialized views.
**Rows in shard:** ~20.8M (dominated by `mv_abr_name_lookup` 9.04M + `gs_relationships` 3.43M).

**Method / provenance**
- Row counts + kind: `census.csv` (exact `count(*)`, verified 2026-08-14).
- Table columns: `columns.csv`. **Matview columns are NOT in `information_schema.columns`** — I pulled all 83 from `pg_attribute` directly (verified).
- Matview base-table dependencies: pulled from `pg_rewrite`/`pg_depend` (verified) — see `Notable clusters`.
- FKs: `foreign_keys.csv` (declared constraints only; most cross-table links here are **undeclared**).
- Refresh status: `SELECT DISTINCT mv_name FROM mv_refresh_log` (verified) + reading `scripts/refresh-views-v2.mjs`, `scripts/refresh-youth-justice-report-cache.mjs`.
- Code usage: ripgrep over `grantscope/apps/web/src`, `grantscope/scripts`, `JusticeHub/src` (excluding `database.types.ts`, which lists every table and inflates matches).
- ~22 targeted `LIMIT 2–3` / aggregate spot-checks against the live DB on the biggest and least-obvious objects. Everything else is inferred from column names + name + code usage, and is marked as such where it matters.

**Type-hazard summary (verified):** within this shard every ABN-like column is `text` (`gs_entities.abn`, `grantconnect_awards.recipient_abn`, `justice_funding.recipient_abn`, all `mv_*.abn`) — no text-vs-bigint mismatch *inside* the shard. Absence is expressed as `NULL`, not `''` (checked on `gs_entities` and `justice_funding`). `postcode`, `lga_code`, `sa2_code`, `state` are all `text`. Entity FKs are `uuid` (`gs_entities.id`); the human-readable `gs_id` is `text` (`AU-ABN-<abn>` / `AU-ORIC-<icn>`), and **both are used as join keys in different matviews** — `mv_award_winner_by_theme.gs_entity_id` is `text` while `grantconnect_awards.gs_entity_id` is `uuid`. That is the one real type mismatch found.

## Notable clusters

### 1. The entity spine (`gs_*`) — 4.06M live rows + 1.46M rows of backup cruft
`gs_entities` (609,448) is the join hub for essentially every other object in the database; `gs_relationships` (3,429,184) is the edge table; `gs_entity_aliases` (16,646) provides name recall; `gs_graph_completeness_log` (169) is its QA telemetry.
Attached to it are **seven backup tables totalling 1,462,763 rows** (`gs_entities_lga_backup_20260808/09/09b/09c`, `gs_entities_reason_backup_20260809b`, `gs_entities_state_backup_20260809`, `gs_entities_dedup_backup_20260809`), all created during the 2026-08-08/09 LGA attribution rebuild, all with **zero references in either codebase**. They are 2.4× the size of the table they back up.

### 2. Materialized-view refresh reality — verified, and worse than it looks
- 98 matviews exist DB-wide; **83 are in this shard**.
- `mv_refresh_log` has ever seen **44 distinct objects** (38 matviews + 6 views).
- 15 more (`mv_yj_report_*`) are refreshed by `scripts/refresh-youth-justice-report-cache.mjs`, which does not log.
- That leaves **~30 matviews in this shard with no known refresh path**, including `mv_entity_total_funding` (94K), `mv_foundation_regranting` (85K), `mv_trustee_grantee_chain` (79K), `mv_fy_donation_contracts` (50K), `mv_funding_outcomes_summary` (39K), `mv_board_power` (38K) and the whole `mv_foundation_landscape_*` family.
- **`mv_person_entity_network` is listed in `refresh-views-v2.mjs` but has never appeared in `mv_refresh_log`**, and two scheduled matviews are built on it.
- **Staleness propagates**: `mv_foundation_scores` is refresh-scheduled but three of its five inputs are not.

### 3. The person / director layer — nine overlapping matviews, ~2.2M rows
`mv_person_entity_network` (336,444) · `mv_person_entity_crosswalk` (331,239) · `mv_person_identity_network` (328,939) · `mv_person_identity_influence` (241,269) · `mv_person_identity_influence_v2` (241,260) · `mv_person_network` (237,990) · `mv_person_influence` (237,340) · `mv_board_interlocks` (39,757) · `mv_board_power` (38,199), plus the tiny `mv_person_cross_system` (282), `mv_multi_board_persons` (1), `mv_board_contractor_links` (4), `mv_board_donor_links` (2).
Three generations are visible: name-normalised (`person_name_normalised`) → identity-resolved (`identity_key`) → attributed (`_v2`). **The newest and most correct generation is the least maintained.** All of them are still topped by nominee blocks (verified: "Mark Smith", 689 boards, `is_nominee_block=true`) — the `MAX_PLAUSIBLE_BOARDS` cap must stay.

### 4. Philanthropy (`mv_foundation_*`, `mv_trustee_*`) — 13 matviews
`mv_foundation_grantees` (15,003) is the base; `mv_foundation_trends` (53,985) is the time series; `mv_foundation_regranting` (85,401) is the two-hop chain; `mv_trustee_grantee_chain` (79,535) is the conflict-of-interest detector; `mv_foundation_scores` (1,753) is the composite. The four `mv_foundation_landscape_*` objects are an **abandoned dashboard** — no refresh, no code references.

### 5. Political influence (`mv_donation_*`, `mv_donor_*`, `mv_fy_*`, `mv_temporal_*`, `mv_revolving_door`)
Six objects, ~294K rows, all derived from `political_donations` × `austender_contracts`. `mv_donation_contract_timing` (232,474) is the pair-level expansion and must not be summed; `mv_donor_contract_crossref` (2,175) is the safe aggregate; `mv_fy_donation_contracts` (50,685) is the FY-aligned variant nobody uses; `mv_temporal_summary` (158) and `mv_revolving_door` (6,976) sit on top.

### 6. Youth-justice report cache (`mv_yj_report_*`) — 15 objects, ~5,300 rows
A purpose-built snapshot layer behind the state youth-justice reports (`scripts/refresh-youth-justice-report-cache.mjs`, `scripts/build-youth-justice-report-snapshot.mjs`). `mv_yj_report_coverage` is a **one-row honest coverage manifest** and is the closest thing in the database to the "see the gaps" surface Ben is asking for.

### 7. JusticeHub-owned families
- **`jr_*` (14 objects)** — justice-reinvestment site evidence + consent workflow. **7 of 14 are empty** (`jr_outcomes`, `jr_evidence_drafts`, `jr_outcome_evidence_links`, `jr_publication_snapshots`, `jr_review_decisions`), `jr_site_authorities` has 1 row, and the three that *are* well populated (`jr_site_research_items` 690, `jr_site_impact_figures` 132, `jr_site_research` 44) have **no code reading them**. The workflow was built from both ends and never joined in the middle.
- **`justice_matrix_*` + `jm_*` (16 objects)** — a strategic-litigation corpus (367 cases, 67 campaigns, 48 sources) plus a RAG Q&A surface that has answered essentially nothing (`jm_answers` = 1).
- Plus `justicehub_nodes`, `mentor_profiles`, `mentorships`, `international_programs`, `historical_inquiries`, `money_flows`, `lga_cross_system_stats`, `locations`, `media_assets`, `media_items`, `messages`, `metrics`, `member_actions`, `kiosk_control_signals`.

### 8. Goods / ACT operations (`goods_*`, `ghl_*`, `goal*`, `idea_*`, `invoice_*`)
`goods_communities` (1,542) is the real asset; `goods_supply_routes` (23,873) is a generated cross-product; five `goods_*` tables are empty, including both halves of the capital-allocation model (`goods_route_allocations`, `goods_cost_allocation_decisions`). The `ghl_*` family is a CRM mirror and a de-facto crosswalk hub (`ghl_contacts` carries 4 different external ids).

### 9. Agent / knowledge layer (`knowledge_*`, `memory_episodes`, `intelligence_*`, `llm_usage`, `learned_thresholds`)
~28K rows of embeddings, episodes and insights. `knowledge_chunks` (19,413) and `memory_episodes` (4,587) hold private content.

### 10. Grants pipeline (`grant_*`, `grantconnect_awards`)
`grantconnect_awards` (291,264 awarded Commonwealth grants, 72.4% entity-linked) is a **major, well-populated, undocumented dataset**. `grant_opportunities` (25,897) is the discovery side, with mixed semantics. The rest (`grant_applications` 33, `grant_assets` 26, `grant_answer_bank` 37) is ACT's own small internal pipeline.
