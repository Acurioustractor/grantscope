## What the file can establish

It identifies WA Government Aboriginal programs, their agency, broad delivery geography, Closing the Gap alignment and whether Aboriginal organisations or ACCOs were involved in delivery. It provides a strong discovery and prioritisation layer for the ACCO Transition Pipeline.

It does not name providers, contract values, contract dates, incumbents, procurement identifiers or transition readiness. Those facts must come from Tenders WA, agency disclosures, contracts and human verification.
