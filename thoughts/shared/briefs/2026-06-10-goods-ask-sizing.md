## The sizing logic (use this every time)

1. **Anchor every ask to a fundable unit**, never a round number:
   - **One community production site: $125K** (kit $100–150K, modelled, vendor quotes pending) — the strongest unit because it IS the QBE transition story
   - **A named community bed run: $850–1,200/bed delivered** (e.g. "100 beds to Palm Island ≈ $100K")
   - **The per-bed philanthropy subsidy: $2,500/bed today falling to $500/bed at 500/yr** — "your dollars per bed halve as we scale; you're funding a machine that needs you less every year"
2. **The match multiplier is the urgency**: every dollar committed in writing before 31 Aug counts toward the QBE match — "a dollar from you this winter is worth two."
3. **Vehicle**: The Butterfly Movement Ltd (Item 1 DGR + PBI since 2012) — tax-deductible now. Never say "Goods is DGR."
